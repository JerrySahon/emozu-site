Privacy

Short version: we only keep what you send us, we use it only to reply, and we delete it when you ask.

Who we are

Emozu is a research project run by its founder, Yury Sakhonchyk, in Yerevan, Armenia. Contact: y@emozu.kids.

What we collect

Only what you send through the form on this site:

  • your role, name, and contact (email or LinkedIn)
  • your message, a link, and a file if you attach one
  • the date and the language of the page

If you write to us by email, we keep that email thread.

The form is for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent to write to us.

Why

To reply to you and to keep the conversation going. We do not sell it, share it for ads, or add you to a mailing list.

No medical data, please

Do not send medical details about a child through the form or by email. If something like that reaches us, we delete it. A home pilot starts only after a separate conversation and written consent.

Where it is stored

Form entries go to a private Google Sheet, and files go to a private Google Drive folder. Email arrives in a private mailbox. Google processes this data for us. Only the founder has access.

How long

As long as the conversation needs it, and no longer than 24 months after the last message. Ask us and we delete it sooner.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no ad trackers. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: page, country, device type and referrer, in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Write to y@emozu.kids. We reply within 30 days.
