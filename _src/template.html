The problem

Today a parent finds out by watching it happen.

This started with one family close to us. Then we read what other parents write, and saw the same picture. The child often cannot say what is wrong. The parent looks for a trigger after the fact, and the next one comes without warning.

Our idea

A soft band. A little friend on top. Serious sensing inside.

Many children pull a medical sensor off. A friend is different. The friend sits on a band, the band reads the body, and the parent gets a quiet message on the phone. No screen on the child and no alarm in the room.

  • Heart rate and HRV
  • Skin conductance, EDA
  • Skin temperature
  • 3-axis motion
  • Battery in the friend
  • No screen, ever

In a 2025 survey of 40 parents of nonverbal children, 67.5% supported a wearable stress detector (Weber et al.).

What it is based on

The body changes first. That can be measured.

Four studies from 2019 to 2026 use a wearable sensor to catch an episode. Three of them predict it, 1 to 10 minutes ahead. The models look for this: heart rate goes up, heart rate variability goes down, and skin conductance bursts come more often.

Hospitals, clinics and classrooms only.

The hard part: about 2 of 3 alerts were false.

A parent who gets pulled away for nothing stops listening. So we do not start with alerts. We start silent: record, label, and look back. “Yesterday at 16:40 it began eight minutes before you noticed” has no false alarms at all. Live alerts come later, and only if the numbers hold.

The gap

99 children in the literature. None of them at home.

Home is where a parent needs the warning most. The research devices the models were trained on left the market: Q-Sensor in 2013, Empatica E4 in 2025.

Roadmap

Where we want to be in 6 to 9 months.

A paper with clinical psychiatrists, on 30 to 50 children, with prototypes we produce ourselves. This is a plan, not traction. Investors: write to us for the one-pager.

NowClosed pilot, one child

Running at home on an off-the-shelf device with the sensor we need. Worn all day, raw signal, labels. About 15 episodes. The question: can we warn ahead, at home?

Step 2Closed beta, six families

Six families are ready, from a parent community of 135 families. Same protocol, more children.

Step 3Protocol and regulator

A scientific advisor turns pilot data into a protocol. Ethics, consent, data rights. FDA Pre-Sub before any claim.

Step 4Prototype production

Our own small batch of bands that give raw signal and that children keep on. Research devices are gone from the market.

Goal · 6 to 9 monthsStudy and paper

With clinical psychiatrists, on 30 to 50 children, by protocol. Then the package for a round.

Open questions we still have to answer: who pays, medical device or wellness, and what the alert threshold should be.
