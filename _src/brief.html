01 · The problem

Today a parent finds out by watching it happen.

The child often cannot say what is wrong. The parent looks for a trigger after the fact, and the next one comes without warning.

  • 47%of 409 parent posts are about hunting for triggersOur tagging, mostly r/Autism_Parenting
  • 68%of autistic children have been aggressive toward a caregiverKanne & Mazurek, 2011
  • 49%tried to run away after age 4Anderson et al., 2012

02 · The idea

A soft band. A little friend on top. Serious sensing inside.

  • Reads the bodyHeart rate and HRV, skin conductance (EDA), skin temperature, motion.
  • Tells the parent quietlyA message on the phone. No screen on the child, no alarm in the room.
  • A friend, not a sensorMany children pull a medical sensor off. A friend snaps on top and holds the battery, so the band stays on.
  • Learns what helpsThe app shows the day and ranks what calms this child, by the body, not by guesswork.

03 · The evidence

The signal is real. Nobody has tested it at home.

The hard part

About 2 of 3 alerts were false in the one study that counts them. A parent pulled away for nothing stops listening. So we start silent: record, label, look back. Live alerts come only if the numbers hold.

04 · Why now

The models arrived. The devices left the market.

  1. 2013Q-Sensor stopped
  2. 2019First model, 20 children
  3. 202370 children, 4 hospitals
  4. 2025Empatica E4 retired
  5. NowNo family device gives raw signal and home data. That is the gap.

05 · The plan

6 to 9 months to a study and a paper.

  1. Now · closed pilot, one childAt home, an off-the-shelf device, worn all day. About 15 episodes. Can we warn ahead?
  2. Step 2 · closed beta, six familiesReady, from a parent community of 135 families.
  3. Step 3 · protocol and regulatorEthics, consent, data rights. FDA Pre-Sub before any claim.
  4. Step 4 · our own prototypesA small batch of bands that give raw signal and that children keep on.
  5. Goal · study and paperWith clinical psychiatrists, on 30 to 50 children, by protocol.

This is a plan, not traction. Open questions: who pays, medical device or wellness, the alert threshold.
